# 3. “Kitni medicine plant par dalni hai?” — important correction

PlantGuard ko blindly:

> “Plant #10 par 8 ml medicine daalo”

nahi bolna chahiye.

Pesticide/fungicide application normally depend karegi:

- Crop
- Disease
- Product formulation
- Disease severity
- Crop growth stage
- Area
- Spray-water volume
- Canopy
- weather
- official registered label
- pre-harvest interval
- application limitations

India mein CIB&RC/DPPQ&S **registered pesticide uses** publish karta hai, aur ICAR advisories crop-specific management recommendations deti hain. 

Isliye PlantGuard mein:

```text
Detected Disease
        ↓
Crop + severity + location
        ↓
Official Treatment Knowledge Base
        ↓
Registered option found?
    YES             NO
     ↓               ↓
Treatment           Expert review
recommendation      recommended
```

### User se inputs

Example:

```text
Field Area: 1 acre
OR
Number of plants: 150
OR
Tank Capacity: 15 L
Crop Stage: flowering
```

Then PlantGuard **official label rate ko calculate** karega.

Fruit trees ke kuch official IPM recommendations actual per-tree spray-water quantities bhi use karte hain, so per-plant calculation only tab hona chahiye jab authoritative source aise specify kare. 

### Golden rule

**PlantGuard medicine/dose invent nahi karega.**

Recommendation must be:

> `AI diagnosis + verified agricultural database/rules`

not:

> `LLM imagination`.

# 32. Candidate research novelty — strongest direction

Existing systems already have:

- plant disease AI
- treatment
- weather prediction
- sensor fusion
- continual learning

So professor ko **“kisi ne kabhi nahi banaya”** mat bolna.

Quick patent review mein image-based future disease prediction, sensor disease-risk models and even multimodal precision spraying already appear in prior art. 

PlantGuard ka strongest candidate contribution:

# **Uncertainty/Disagreement-Driven Adaptive Evidence Acquisition**

Example:

```text
Vision AI:
Late Blight = 85%

Sensor AI:
Late Blight risk = 14%
```

PlantGuard blindly decision nahi lega.

```text
Models disagree
     ↓
Increase sensor sampling
     ↓
Request/capture another image
     ↓
Check sensor health
     ↓
Re-run diagnosis
```

Normal:

```text
Low uncertainty
→ normal sampling
```

Suspicious:

```text
High uncertainty
→ higher sampling
→ more evidence
```

Possible measurable benefits:

- false positives ↓
- false alerts ↓
- unnecessary transmission ↓
- confidence ↑
- power/network usage ↓

**Patent novelty guarantee nahi**, but ye specific mechanism proper prior-art search ke liye strong candidate hai.

# 59. Patent strategy

Very important:

### Before public disclosure

Do:
- prior-art search
- university IPR consultation
- decide patent-worthy mechanism.

India Patent Act excludes:
- mere arrangement of known devices
- method of agriculture/horticulture
- computer programme per se/algorithm

under Section 3 clauses including 3(f), 3(h), 3(k). 

Therefore don't try:

> “Patent: AI identifies plant disease.”

Candidate technical invention:

> **A distributed sensor-visual diagnostic architecture that dynamically changes sensing/image acquisition based on cross-modal uncertainty, validates sensor integrity before fusion and triggers evidence reacquisition when modalities disagree.**

Then patent professional/IPR Cell decides whether claims survive prior-art/legal examination.
